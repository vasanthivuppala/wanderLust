# 🌍 ExploreEase — AI-Powered Travel Planning & Staycation Booking

ExploreEase is a full-stack travel platform that combines **AI-powered trip planning, destination discovery, and staycation booking** in one place. It helps travelers plan personalized trips while allowing hosts to list and manage their properties.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🤖 **AI Trip Planner:** Generate personalized travel plans based on destination, budget, trip duration, and preferences.
- 💰 **Budget-Friendly Planning:** Get recommendations tailored to your available budget.
- 🎒 **Smart Packing Suggestions:** Receive packing recommendations based on your destination and trip requirements.
- 📍 **Destination Discovery:** Explore attractions and places to visit around your destination.
- 🏡 **Staycation Discovery & Booking:** Explore accommodation options and book your preferred stay.
- 🔐 **Secure Authentication:** Sign up and log in to access personalized features.
- ⭐ **Reviews & Ratings:** Use traveler feedback to evaluate staycation options.
- 🏠 **Host Dashboard:** Allow hosts to add and manage their property listings.

## 💡 What Makes ExploreEase Different?

- **One-Stop Travel Solution:** Combines trip planning, staycation discovery, and booking in a single platform.
- **Personalized Travel Assistance:** Uses an AI travel agent to help users organize trips according to their needs.
- **Budget-Conscious Recommendations:** Helps travelers make choices that fit their spending limits.
- **Traveler and Host Support:** Serves both travelers looking for stays and hosts who want to showcase their properties.

## 🚀 How It Works

1. **Enter Your Preferences:** Choose your destination, budget, trip duration, and interests.
2. **Generate a Travel Plan:** Get a personalized itinerary with suggested attractions and packing tips.
3. **Explore Staycations:** Browse accommodation options at your destination.
4. **Book Your Stay:** Select a suitable property and book it through ExploreEase.
5. **Manage Listings:** Hosts can add and update their property details.

## 💻 Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, EJS
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
- **Database:** MongoDB
- **AI:** AI-powered travel planning agent

## 🎯 Project Goal

To simplify travel planning by bringing **AI-based personalization, budget-conscious itineraries, accommodation discovery, and booking** together in one convenient platform.
